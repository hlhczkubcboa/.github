## 🛡️ Real talk — what automation can't fix

- 📉 **Stops and limits protect — they don't predict.** Your stop executes at -10% exactly as told; that's risk management, not a crystal ball
- 💸 **Slippage exists.** DEX fills aren't CEX fills — the bot caps it, but on thin pools the cap can mean "order skipped", and that's the right outcome
- ⏰ **Missed pumps happen.** Limit at $2,800 while the market runs to $3,200? The order never filled. That's trading
- ⚠️ **Never automate money you can't afford to lose.** The bot follows your plan perfectly — including a bad one
